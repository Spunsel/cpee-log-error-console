CPEE Conversational Process Modeling with LLMs

CPEE supports conversational process modeling with large language models (LLMs). Instead of manually defining every process detail, users can describe intended behavior in natural language and iteratively refine the process through dialogue.

The LLM can assist with structure suggestions, model corrections, and explanations of modeling decisions. This enables faster iteration and makes process modeling more accessible, especially during early design and debugging phases.

In this questionnaire, you will inspect process behavior and identify issues using two complementary views: raw execution logs and an interactive debugging console.

Please focus on why the process behaves as shown, then select the answer that best matches your diagnosis.
